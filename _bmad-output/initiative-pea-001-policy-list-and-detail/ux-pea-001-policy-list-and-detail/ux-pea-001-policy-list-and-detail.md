## Design delivery

The policyholder mockups use a restrained policy-record visual language: warm paper surfaces, fine separators, evergreen actions, and serif record headings with compact sans-serif labels. Status remains explicit in text, and policy facts and beneficiary allocations stay central to the page hierarchy. The active, expired, unavailable, and intake-boundary states preserve the non-binding endorsement boundary.
