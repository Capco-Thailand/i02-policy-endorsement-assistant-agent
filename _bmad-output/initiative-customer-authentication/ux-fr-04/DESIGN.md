# FR-04 visual design

Extend the existing policy detail page. Keep the policy heading, structured facts, beneficiaries, and request action in their current order. Add a **Synthetic policy evidence** section after the facts and before the request action. The structured facts remain the primary record; evidence is a cited explanation.

Use the established typography, colors, spacing, border, and focus styles in `design/global.css`. Each source card shows the document title, version, section ID, and a short excerpt. Keep citation metadata as text rather than relying on color or icons. State notices use plain language and remain visible at 320px without horizontal scrolling.

The [browser mockup](../../../design/page/policy-evidence.html) demonstrates available, unresolved, loading, service failure, and session ended states. It contains synthetic examples only.
